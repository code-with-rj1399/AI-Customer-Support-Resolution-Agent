# Refund Policy

## Automatic refunds

Customers are eligible for an automatic refund when an order is delayed by at least 3 calendar days and the original payment has been successfully captured.

The refund is limited to the amount actually captured for the order.

## Manual review

Orders delayed by fewer than 3 days require manual review. Orders with a payment that is not captured are not eligible for an automatic refund.

## Refund processing

Refunds must be issued against the original payment. The support platform must use an idempotency key when creating a refund to prevent duplicate refunds.
