# Shipping Policy

## Delivery delays

An order is considered delayed when the expected delivery date has passed and the order has not been delivered.

Customers should receive status information before a resolution is offered. For delays of 3 or more calendar days, the refund policy may permit an automatic refund when payment is captured.

## Delivery exceptions

Weather, carrier disruption, customs, and incorrect address information can affect delivery dates. These facts should be verified from the order and delivery systems rather than inferred from this document.
